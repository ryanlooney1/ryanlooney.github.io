The challenge

Ad CTAs are the most important content at Meta from a business perspective, with billions of daily impressions driving the bulk of ad revenue. But no team formally owned the short list of available ad CTAs. For decades, "Learn more" was the default CTA because it generally applied to most ads.

It was also the worst-performing CTA. User research showed people found "Learn more" vague and uninformative, leaving them unsure of what would happen after tapping. At Meta's scale, even a small improvement to the default ad CTA would compound into enormous impact. That was the opening.

Thanks to his end-to-end ownership—from identifying the problem and analyzing the test results to securing leadership buy-in—the new CTA successfully launched and drove measurable value for both users and advertisers."
Allie Rubin, Product Manager, Instagram

My approach

Proved the opportunity

I mapped CTA ownership across monetization, product, and content teams and confirmed the gap was real. Then I authored the foundational research analyzing ad CTAs, and started a CTA Road Show to build consensus and earn investment.

Built the dynamic CTA system

Combining user research, performance data, and LLM-assisted ideation, I generated and tested 1,000+ CTA variants, then hand-curated the winners into a system of 100+ variants across 20 base types and 7 languages. This served the best-performing CTA for each ad impression in real time, guided by principles I set and validated.

Launched the new global default

I proposed "See details" as the new default ad CTA for all of Meta, and validated it through static testing across Instagram and Facebook. I drove the cross-functional launch end to end, and codified it into a replicable 8-step methodology. It launched globally in March 2026.

AI in practice

With this project as inspiration, I created an AI Pod to optimize other CTAs across Instagram. As the first Content Designer to lead an AI Pod, I built a cross-functional AI agent team that saved my human partners time and energy, generating research and code faster than previously possible.

Key insight

The most scalable thing a content designer can do is set the standard by hand, through research and rigorous testing, then encode it through AI. I hand-crafted 1,000+ CTAs to define what "good" looks like, then turned that judgment into systems that generate it.
